Merit Points COR

In [Final Fantasy XI](https://www.google.com/search?q=final+fantasy+xi&kgmid=/m/026653), spending your Corsair merits focuses on maximizing your party buff duration, ability recasts, and utility. [1, 2]

## Category: Group 1 (Job Ability / Combat Enhancements)

- Phantom Roll Recast: 5/5 — Reduces the recast timer of your Phantom Rolls by 2 seconds per upgrade (-10 seconds total). Essential for smooth rolling.
- Quick Draw Recast: 5/5 — Lowers the recast timer of your Quick Draw elemental shots, giving you faster access to Light Shot, Dark Shot, and skillchain/magic burst utility. [2, 3, 4, 5]

## Category: Group 2 (Job Abilities / Traits)

- Winning Streak: 3/3 — Increases the duration of your Phantom Rolls by 20 seconds per rank (+60 seconds total, bringing base 5-minute rolls up). This is the standard choice for general play and most group content.
- Fold: 1/5 (or more depending on taste) — Putting at least 1/5 here unlocks the Fold ability, letting you wipe aw-ay an unwanted roll or a bust. Most players stop at 1/5 unless they frequently push risky double-ups.
- Snake Eye / Loaded Deck: Generally left at 0/5 unless you have a niche build or specific Gaol/Niche strategy that requires frequent resetting via Loaded Deck. [1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]

## Category: Combat and Attributes

- Marksmanship Skill: 8/8 — Max out your gun skill to help your ranged accuracy and damage on weapon skills like Leaden Salute and Last Stand.
- Sword Skill: 8/8 — Max out if you frequently melee with Naegling and spam Savage Blade.
- Attributes (STR / AGI / DEX): Prioritize STR (for physical and hybrid weapon skills) or AGI (for ranged attacks and magic weapon skills like Wildfire / Leaden Salute), depending on your gear build. [5]
