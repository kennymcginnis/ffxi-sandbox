As a Corsair / Dancer (COR/DNC) main—and knowing that was your very first 99 back in the day—you already bring massive utility to your party through phantom rolls, step debuffs, and situational waltzes. Since your job lets you comfortably pivot between Ranged DPS, Melee DPS, and support, your Trust setup needs to be rock solid to let you focus on your positioning and shot/roll cycles.
Because you are playing COR/DNC, you should tailor your Alter Ego Points around the following specific priorities: [1, 2]

## 1. Maximize HP, VIT, and INT (High Priority)

As a COR, you will frequently pull hate if you land a massive Leaden Salute or Wildfire. If your Tank Trust (like August or Valaineral) fails to hold enmity or gets wiped by an AoE magical attack, you become the target.

-
- HP & VIT: Your Tank Trusts desperately need these to take physical hits while you machine-gun your ranged attacks or build TP on the flank.
- INT: Crucial for your Healer Trusts (like Yoran-Oran or Apururu) and support casters. High-end endgame monsters love to spam heavy magic AoEs that can easily one-shot fragile cloth-wearing casters.
-

## 2. Magic Skills & MND (Medium-High Priority)

-
- Magic Skills: If your Healer Trusts miss their dispels or your support Trusts miss their debuffs, your clear times slow down dramatically. Boosting Magic Skills ensures their magical accuracy scales effectively against high-level targets. [1]
- MND: Directly increases the raw healing potency of your White Mage Trusts and boosts their magic defense. Since COR has limited defensive abilities outside of DNC sub-job waltzes, keeping your primary healers functioning at peak efficiency is vital. [3]
-

## 3. Combat Skills (Medium Priority)

-
- If you rely on physical DPS/Skillchain partner Trusts (like Ayame to open skillchains for your Leaden Salute, or Shantotto II to magic burst off them), they need raw Combat Skills. This prevents them from constantly missing physical swings on high-level content, ensuring your skillchain setups stay perfectly on time. [1]
-

## 4. STR, DEX, and AGI (Low Priority)

-
- As a COR, you are the primary damage dealer in your Trust party. Investing points to make your Trusts hit marginally harder with physical melee swings provides very low returns. Save these stats for much later down the line when you have points to burn.
-

## ⚠️ A Note on the Max Level Cap Increase

Keep in mind that while the system originally launched with a limit of 50 levels per node, the September 2026 version update increased the maximum point allocation to 60 levels per node. It now takes a total of 210 points to fully max out a single stat to level 60. Stick to the "spread" strategy—bringing HP, VIT, and INT up to level 20 or 30 first—before you try pushing anything to the new level 60 cap.

To allocate your points, you must first be standing inside a Mog House or a Mog Garden. The allocation menu is completely locked and will not appear if you are out in the field. [1, 2]

## Step-by-Step Menu Path

1.  Head inside your Mog House or Mog Garden.
2.  Change your main job to your level 99 Corsair (you must be on a level 99 job to use the menu).
3.  Open the Main Menu.
4.  Select Status (or Profile, depending on your UI layout).
5.  Select Alter Ego Points. [1, 3, 4]

From there, the stat grid will open up, allowing you to spend your accumulated points. [5]
Note: If you follow this path while inside your Mog House and the option is completely missing, it usually means you are using an out-of-date custom user interface or icon mod. Disabling the UI mod or restoring the vanilla FFXI ROM files will fix it. [6]

Alter Ego Points are a time-gated resource. Unlike standard Job Points, you cannot grind them continuously by killing monsters. Instead, you earn them by completing specific periodic activities and consuming item tokens from your physical inventory. [1, 2, 3]
The primary ways to farm and collect more points include:

## 1. Monthly Records of Eminence (The Main Repeatable Source)

The standard, recurring method to accumulate points is by keeping up with your monthly Records of Eminence (RoE) objectives. Under your RoE ledger, completing monthly tasks rewards you with specific tokens: [1, 2, 3]

-
- Trust Primer: Consuming this item awards 20 Alter Ego Points. You can obtain these regularly through the monthly [Deeds of Heroism](http://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alter_ego_points) objectives. [1, 4, 5]
-

## 2. Massive One-Time Quest Boosts

If you haven't completed your foundational Records of Eminence questlines, there are huge chunks of points waiting for you: [5, 6]

-
- Marjory's Thesis (200 Points): If you complete the Records of Eminence quest objective "Way Over Capacity", go back and talk to Marjory (H-5) in Ru'Lude Gardens. She will hand you this item, which grants an immediate 200 points upon consumption. Note: If you completed this quest prior to the system's launch, simply talk to her again to claim it retrospectively. [1]
-

## 3. Limited-Time Events & Campaigns

Square Enix regularly introduces seasonal catch-up mechanics and group activities to inject more points into the economy: [1, 2]

-
- Trust Tome (100 Points): These high-value items are occasionally handed out during special login campaigns or via specific event rewards.
- Vana'Bout: Participating in the server-wide Vana'Bout cooperative events rewards active players with extra point-granting tokens. [1, 2, 7]
-

Whenever you acquire any of these physical items (Primers, Tomes, or Theses), simply use them directly from your inventory to add the points straight to your total. You can then step back into your Mog House to distribute them. [1, 3, 8, 9]
