THF Merit Points

In [Final Fantasy XI](https://www.google.com/search?q=final+fantasy+xi&kgmid=/m/026653) ([FFXI)](https://www.google.com/search?q=ffxi%29&kgmid=/m/026653), a Thief (THF) spends merit points primarily on Group 1 and Group 2 job-specific categories, alongside general combat/attribute merits. [1, 2]

## Group 1 Job Merits

- Triple Attack Rate: 5/5 (Capped) — Essential for maximizing raw damage output, melee hits, and TP gain. [3]
- Sneak Attack Recast / Trick Attack Recast: Split the remaining points depending on your playstyle:
- Trick Attack (5/5): Favored in parties if you want shorter cooldowns on enmity redirection.
  - Sneak Attack (5/5): Favored for solo play or high critical-hit weapon skill rotations. [4]

## Group 2 Job Merits

- Feint: 5/5 (Capped) — Critically important. It drops the target's evasion significantly [-150 evasion penalty] and boosts your Treasure Hunter activation chance per merit point [+25% per additional point, max +125%].
- Assassin's Charge: 1/5 or higher — Guarantees a Triple Attack on your next swing/weaponskill with a chance to roll into a Quadruple Attack.
- Aura Steal: 1/5 — Put at least one point here so you can dispel buffs off enemies.
- Ambush: 0/5 (generally skipped or avoided as outdated compared to other options). [1, 3, 5]

## General & Combat Merits (Priority Order)

1.  Dagger Skill: Max to 16/16 (or current cap) to ensure top-tier accuracy and weapon skill performance with daggers like Rudra's Storm or Mandalic Stab.
2.  Evasion Skill: Maximize to support your high-evasion survival build.
3.  Dexterity / Strength: Invest in DEX first for critical hit rate and main dagger/WS scaling, followed by STR.
4.  Max Merit Capacity: Expand your merit point storage limit (up to 75 total) so you can stack points efficiently during group farm sessions. [6, 7, 8, 9]

In [Final Fantasy XI](https://www.google.com/search?q=final+fantasy+xi&kgmid=/m/026653), Thief (THF) Job Points (JP) are unlocked at level 99 after obtaining the Job Breaker key item and spending Capacity Points (CP) (1 JP per 30,000 CP). [1] 
## THF Job Point Categories
Categories can be upgraded up to 20 ranks via the Status > Job Points menu: [1, 2] 

* 
* Ability/Effect Enhancements: Enhances traits and abilities such as Perfect Dodge Effect (+3 magic evasion per rank), Larceny Duration (+1s per rank), Sneak Attack (+1% DEX bonus per rank), Trick Attack (+1% AGI bonus per rank), and Steal Recast (-2s per rank). [2] 
* Combat Utilities: Upgrades include Mug Effect (steals HP equal to 5% DEX/AGI per rank), Despoil Effect (steals 2% target TP per rank), Conspirator Effect (+1 accuracy per rank), Bully Effect (+1% intimidation chance per rank), and Triple Attack Effect (+1 physical attack per rank). [2, 3] 
* 

## Key Gift Milestones
Investing total Job Points unlocks permanent stats and Gifts: [3, 4] 

* 
* Early Milestones: 5 JP (+4 Defense, +5% CP bonus), 10 JP (+7 Attack), 20 JP (+10 Evasion), 25 JP (+7% CP bonus), 30 JP (+5 Accuracy), and 45 JP (+5 Magic Evasion).
* Progression: Unlocks Superior 1 (Su1) gear at 50 JP, continuing to enhanced stats, Treasure Hunter bonuses, and Job Master status at 2,100 JP. [1, 3]
